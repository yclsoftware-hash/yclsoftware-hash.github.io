Fun Math

Math learning game

Practice math the fun way.

Math questions turn into games: answer correctly and your character attacks, answer wrong and the enemy strikes back. Levels get harder step by step, from the four operations up to more advanced questions.

Game modes

Three ways to practice

1

Battle

Your correct answers attack your character's enemy. Answer wrong and the enemy attacks you.

2

Reach the number

Get a target number for your level and reach it by combining numbers with the operations.

3

Listen and find

Listen to numbers spoken in English and pick the right one while you learn them.

What you practice

All four operations

Addition and subtraction

Warm up with sums and differences, then speed up.

Multiplication and division

Move on to times tables and division questions.

Levels that grow with you

As the levels progress, the questions become more difficult.

Learn by repeating

Replay a level as often as you like to get better at it.
