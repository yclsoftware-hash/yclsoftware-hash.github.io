Fun Math

Math learning games for kids

Math kids love to play.

Fun Math turns practice into play: 9 colorful math games where solving is the game itself, not an extra quiz. Match candies that add up to 10, send jelly monsters through the True or False gate, build bridges with the right shapes and balance the scale.

9 games in one app

Every level is a small puzzle

Every puzzle is real practice, with 30 levels in every game.

Number Candy

Connect sweets that make 10 and fill the ten frame.

True or False Gate

Is 7 + 5 = 12? Swipe the monster to the right gate.

Bridge Builder

Shapes, turning, symmetry and angles.

Target Number Rally

Plan your sum with +3, −2 and ×2 tokens.

Elevator Master

Move on the number line in the fewest steps.

Balance Scale

Equal sides, the first step toward equations.

Clock Town

Read the clock: o'clock, half past, quarter and minutes.

Money Shop

Pay and give the right change.

Listen to the Number

Hear it in English and find it, from 1 to 100.

Made for learning

Learn by doing, step by step

Real math topics

Addition and subtraction, counting, place value, shapes, time and money.

Mistakes that teach

Wrong moves are not punished: a ten frame, number line or blocks show why.

Concrete to abstract

From objects to pictures to numbers, one step at a time.

Bosses and bonus rounds

A boss every 10 levels and a True or False bonus round.
