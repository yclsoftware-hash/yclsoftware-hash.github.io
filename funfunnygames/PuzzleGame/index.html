Draw Rescue

Ball physics game

Draw a line. Save the ball. Roll it into the right cup.

Colored balls fall from above and each one has to reach the cup of its own color before your ink runs out. One simple move, real physics and a fresh challenge in every level.

How to play

Four simple moves

1

Draw

Drag one finger to draw a line. Your ink is limited and the line fades after a moment.

2

Bounce

Every ball rolls with real physics: slopes, bounces, spin and speed all matter.

3

Match

Send each ball into the cup that matches its color.

4

Combo

Rescue balls back to back to build a combo and multiply your score.

Always something new

A drawing game that keeps changing

Levels keep being added, so there is always one more to try.

Special pieces

Glass that has to break first, switch colors, ice shells, bombs and jokers.

Obstacles

Pegs, ramps, spinning gears, wind bands, funnels, crates, lasers and locked cups.

Different lines

Slick, bouncy, sticky, painting and permanent lines change how every level plays.

Different goals

Fill the cups, break the glass, hit a combo, finish flawless, paint the floor or find the treasure cup.

New worlds

From candy valleys to the clouds

Every world brings new toys to play with, and bosses are waiting along the way.

Amusement park

Bumpers, balloons to pop, cups on rails, portals and festival levels full of surprise balls.

Slingshots and swinging cups

Launch balls with a slingshot, open gates with buttons and drop them into cups that sway like a Ferris wheel.

Sky worlds

Upside-down gravity, slow moon gravity, big fans and birds that carry the balls away.

Black holes and magnet lines

Black holes pull the balls in, while a magnet line pulls them toward your drawing.

Special balls and super combos

Magnet, lightning, time and rainbow balls help you out, and two of them can merge into a super combo.

Boss levels

Face the Juggler and the Storm Bird and bring them down with your cups and special balls.
