This privacy policy applies to the FunCopter: Helicopter Games app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Fun Games & Funny Games (collectively, the "Application"). Fun Games & Funny Games is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Package name: plane.helicopter_bird_flying.Wing

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • your mobile operating system you use

Google Play Games Services and cloud save

The Application uses Google Play Games Services so that you can sign in with your Google account, unlock achievements, submit scores to the all-time and weekly leaderboards and keep your game progress in the cloud.

  • When you sign in, Google provides the Application with your Play Games player ID and your public player name. The Service Provider never receives your Google password.
  • Leaderboards show the public player names, avatars and scores of other players, as provided by Google Play Games Services. Your score and public player name are shown to other players in the same way.
  • Achievement progress and leaderboard scores are stored and displayed by Google Play Games Services and are handled under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • Cloud save: if you sign in, your game progress (levels and islands, gold balance, helicopters and their upgrades, cosmetics, chests, quests, daily rewards and achievement rewards, plus the record of purchases already delivered) is saved with Google Play Games Saved Games. Only this Application can read this save; the Service Provider cannot see any other data in your Google account. No payment, contact or location data is stored there.
  • You can withdraw this access at any time from your Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Removing access stops any further cloud save; the progress already on your device is not affected.

How Google handles this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Gameplay analytics

The Application uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players progress through the game, so that levels, rewards and prices that are too hard or unclear can be improved.

  • What is recorded: gameplay events such as starting, finishing or failing a level (with the island and level number, the helicopter used and its level, the time spent, stars and gold earned, and the cause of a crash), rewarded ad offers and rewards, gold spent in the in-game shop, opened chests, daily rewards, leaderboard visits, and the product identifier of an in-app purchase. A few summary values are stored as user properties: highest level reached, active helicopter, number of helicopters owned and whether a purchase has been made.
  • The Firebase SDK also records basic technical information on its own: an app-instance identifier generated for this installation, first-open and session events, device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate country derived from your IP address.
  • What is not recorded: your name, email address, Play Games player ID, payment details or precise location. Analytics data is not used for advertising or ad personalization.
  • This data is used only in aggregate to improve the game. You can stop further collection by uninstalling the Application. How Google processes this data is described in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Crash reports and remote settings

  • Firebase Crashlytics: if the Application crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent so the problem can be fixed. A report contains the error and the code location, device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier. It does not contain your name, email address or game account.
  • Firebase Remote Config: the Application downloads game settings (for example, balance values and whether an update is available) from Firebase. To do this, Firebase uses a random installation identifier; no personal data is sent.
  • How Google processes this data is described in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

In-app purchases

The Application offers optional in-app purchases of gold packs. Gold can also be earned by playing, and you can play without buying anything.

  • All purchases are processed by Google Play. Your card number, billing address and other payment details are entered in Google Play and are never shared with the Service Provider.
  • To deliver an item, the Application receives the purchase confirmation from Google Play on your device (product, order ID, purchase token and purchase status). The Service Provider does not run its own purchase server.
  • Google Play gives the Service Provider the order information it needs for refunds and accounting, for example the order number, product, price, date and the buyer’s country, state or postal code. How Google handles payment data is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.
  • Your gold balance and the list of purchases already delivered are stored on your device and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, in your cloud save as described above.
  • The purchase flow uses Unity In-App Purchasing, a library from Unity Technologies that connects the game to Google Play Billing.
  • Parents can require approval for every purchase in the Google Play settings (purchase authentication) or with Google Family Link.

Advertising

The Application shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you tap a button that offers a reward, such as continuing a level, extra gold or opening a chest sooner; ads never start on their own.

  • To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your device’s Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Notifications

With your permission, the Application shows notifications, for example when a chest is ready to open, when the weekly leaderboard contest has ended, or a reminder after a few days without playing. These notifications are scheduled on your device; no data is sent to the Service Provider to show them, and you can turn them off at any time in your device’s app settings.

Ratings and updates

The Application may ask you to rate it using Google Play’s in-app review screen, and may offer an update using Google Play’s in-app update screen. Both are shown by Google Play; the Service Provider does not see your rating, review text or account.
