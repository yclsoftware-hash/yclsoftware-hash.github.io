Google Play Games Services and cloud save

The Application uses Google Play Games Services so that you can sign in with your Google account, unlock achievements and submit scores to leaderboards, and — where the feature is enabled — keep your game progress in your own Google Drive application data folder.

  • When you sign in, Google provides the Application with your Play Games player ID and your public player name. The Service Provider never receives your Google password.
  • Achievement progress and leaderboard scores are stored and displayed by Google Play Games Services and are handled under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • Cloud save: if you sign in, your game progress (completed levels, stars, lifetime counters, remaining lives, diamond and booster balances and in-game settings) is written to the hidden “application data” folder of your own Google Drive. Only this Application can read that folder; the Service Provider cannot see or browse any other file in your Drive. No payment, contact or location data is stored there.
  • You can withdraw this access at any time from your Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Removing access stops any further cloud save; the progress already on your device is not affected.

How Google handles this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.


Gameplay analytics

The Application uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players progress through the levels, so that levels that are too hard or confusing can be improved.

  • What is recorded: gameplay events such as starting, finishing or continuing a level, together with the level number, the attempt number, the time spent on the level and the stars earned. The Firebase SDK also records basic technical information on its own: an app-instance identifier generated for this installation, first-open and session events, device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate country derived from your IP address.
  • What is not recorded: your name, email address, Play Games player ID, payment details or precise location. The advertising ID is not collected for analytics, and analytics data is not used for advertising or ad personalization.
  • This data is used only in aggregate to improve the game. It is not collected while the game runs in automated testing.
  • You can stop further collection by uninstalling the Application. How Google processes this data is described in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

In-app purchases

The Application offers optional in-app purchases: diamonds, which are spent on boosters, and time-limited unlimited lives. You can play without buying anything.

  • All purchases are processed by Google Play. Your card number, billing address and other payment details are entered in Google Play and are never shared with the Service Provider.
  • To deliver an item, the Application receives the purchase confirmation from Google Play on your device (product, order ID, purchase token and purchase status). The Service Provider does not run its own purchase server.
  • Google Play gives the Service Provider the order information it needs for refunds and accounting, for example the order number, product, price, date and the buyer’s country, state or postal code. How Google handles payment data is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.
  • Your diamond and booster balances are stored on your device and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, in your cloud save as described above. The unlimited lives timer is stored only on your device.
  • The purchase flow uses Unity In-App Purchasing, a library from Unity Technologies that connects the game to Google Play Billing.
  • Parents can require approval for every purchase in the Google Play settings (purchase authentication) or with Google Family Link.

Advertising and consent

The Application shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you tap a button that offers a reward, such as an extra life; ads never start on their own.

  • To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • In regions where consent is required by law, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Application shows Google’s consent form when it starts, and ads are requested only after you have made a choice. Ads are then served according to that choice, for example as non-personalized ads.
  • In these regions you can review or change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the main menu. Your choice also applies to gameplay analytics: if you do not allow storing information on your device, analytics storage is turned off.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your device’s Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Notifications
